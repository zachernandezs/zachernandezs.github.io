
# About DotDesk

DotDesk is an app that helps you get the most about your day. Though the use various widgets, you will be able to plan your daily tasks, jot down ideas and notes, map your thoughts, track your time, and focus on what is most important. 

### Features

 - Clock and calendar
 - Daily planner
 - Markdown Notes
 - Task tracker (daily, weekly, bi-weekly)
 - Pomodoro timer
 - Sticky Notes, cards and drawable background
 - Separate desks
 - Connection to Obsidian
